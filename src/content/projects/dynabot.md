## Multi-Dof Dynamic NanoRobotics Inside Electron Microscopes

DyNaBot investigated how to control nanorobot motion in three dimensions inside electron microscopes. Coordinated by FEMTO-ST, the project combined force sensing and digital modeling to support tasks such as manipulating and assembling small components.

My [doctoral research at FEMTO-ST](/projects/nanorobotic-origami/) was related to this work. I studied robotic microassembly and laser actuation of silica structures at an optical fiber tip inside a scanning electron microscope.

ANR lists project **ANR-21-CE10-0016** with a **December 2021** start and a **48-month** duration. [ANR project record](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-21-CE10-0016)
