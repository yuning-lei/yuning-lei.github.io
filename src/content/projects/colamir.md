## Collaborative Agile Miniaturized Robotics for ultra-precise assembly

Coordinated by FEMTO-ST, COLAMIR studied how human operators and miniature robots could work together in industrial microassembly. The project used microforce sensors and haptic feedback to let operators feel the forces involved in handling small components.

During my [doctoral research at FEMTO-ST](/projects/nanorobotic-origami/), I worked on robotic microassembly and attended COLAMIR project meetings.

ANR lists project **ANR-16-CE10-0009** with a **November 2016** start and a **42-month** duration. [ANR project record](https://anr.fr/Project-ANR-16-CE10-0009)
