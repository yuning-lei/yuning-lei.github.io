## Emerging nanoscopy for single entity characterisation

ENSIGN is a Horizon Europe MSCA Staff Exchanges project coordinated by Aarhus University. It aims to combine force, electrical and microwave measurements with optical and electron imaging to characterize individual entities at the nanoscale.

I manage the University of Oldenburg’s participation and serve as the university’s point of contact for the consortium.

The project runs from **1 March 2023 to 28 February 2027** under grant agreement **101086226**. [European Commission CORDIS project record](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101086226)
