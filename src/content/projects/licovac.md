## Liquid Metal Contact Angle Measurement in Vacuum

LiCoVac is a DFG research project at the University of Oldenburg.

The associated research concerns liquid-metal droplet fabrication and contact-angle measurement inside a scanning electron microscope. Publications report droplet fabrication by electromigration and size control through focused ion beam sputtering.

The 2025 journal article acknowledges DFG support for LiCoVac under grant FA 347/59-1.
