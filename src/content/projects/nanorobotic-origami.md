## Nanorobotic Origami Unfolded at the Tip of Optical Fiber

My doctoral research at FEMTO-ST Institute, Besançon, addressed silica origami structures fabricated by ion-induced folding. The work included robotic microassembly, characterization and actuation of these structures at an optical fiber tip inside an SEM.

The research was supervised by Cédric Clévy, Jean-Yves Rauch and Philippe Lutz. I defended the dissertation on 24 March 2023 at Université Bourgogne Franche-Comté.

The thesis investigates focused ion beam fabrication of elastic silica joints and their assembly into polyarticulated structures, with laser actuation delivered through the optical fiber.

The English-language dissertation is available in [HAL, version 1](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04146180v1), deposited on 29 June 2023. Its English title is *Nanorobotic Origami Unfolded at the Tip of Optical Fiber*; HAL’s citation title is *Origami nanorobotique déployé en bout de fibre*.
