The University of Oldenburg and The University of Hong Kong are investigating how to automate AFM measurements on individual nanoplastic particles. The project aims to automate the steps from locating a particle on a sample surface to measuring its size and shape and recording force–distance curves.

I manage the project and supervise research activities at Oldenburg.

The **2025–2026** collaboration is supported through the Germany/Hong Kong Joint Research Scheme. The Hong Kong Research Grants Council lists it as project **G-HKU705/24**.
