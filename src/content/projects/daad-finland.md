The University of Oldenburg and Aalto University set out to study how adhesion and wetting measurements at different scales relate to one another. The project was designed to compare two methods on the same surfaces: scanning droplet adhesion microscopy with water in air and liquid-metal droplet measurements in vacuum.

I managed the project and supervised research activities at Oldenburg. In September 2023, I visited Aalto’s Robotic Instruments Group to discuss the joint research. [AMiR’s visit report](https://uol.de/en/computingscience/amir/news-events-press)

Aalto University lists its project period as **March 2023 – February 2025**. [Aalto University research portal](https://research.aalto.fi/en/projects/daad-mobility/)
