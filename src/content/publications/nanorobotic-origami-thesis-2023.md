This doctoral dissertation concerns silica origami structures, robotic microassembly and actuation at an optical fiber tip in SEM. The original French title is retained in the citation; the English title in my CV is *Nanorobotic Origami Unfolded at the Tip of Optical Fiber*.

The dissertation was defended on 24 March 2023. Its national thesis identifier is **2023UBFCD012**. The DOI identifies the public thesis catalogue record.
