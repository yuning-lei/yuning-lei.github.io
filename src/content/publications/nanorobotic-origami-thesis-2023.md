## Nanorobotic Origami Unfolded at the Tip of Optical Fiber

This thesis studies robotic motion at the nanoscale using laser-actuated silica origami structures at an optical fiber tip inside a scanning electron microscope. The work combines focused ion beam patterning and folding, robotic microassembly, and characterization of polyarticulated structures built from elastic silica joints.
